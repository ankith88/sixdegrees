# SixDegrees Workspace Surface Brief

## Scope and Mode

- **Targets:** `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/components/SixDegreesApp.tsx`
- **Mode:** `operate`

## Task and Constraints

- Enable cinephiles and viewers to explore a film or TV show's creative constellation (2D/3D spatial graph or ranked ledger matrix), switch between three weighting lenses (Vibe & Tropes, Auteur Orbit, Star Constellation), filter entity types, search live global titles, and inspect shared collaborators and regional streaming availability (AU, US, UK).
- Remove raw physics sliders (Spherical Force, Link Distance, Z-Repulsion) and strip all dark-mode AI slop and fake telemetry jargon.

## Direction contract

THESIS: SixDegrees is a daylight-first film monograph and archival relationship registry that replaces dark-mode sci-fi telemetry slop with the tactile clarity of a Criterion Collection essay booklet and numbered spine index.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cotton rag paper ground (`#F6F1E7`), archival card stock (`#EFE8DA`), deep carbon-sepia ink (`#171412`), warm umber secondary ink (`#5C5346`), Criterion crimson primary accent (`#B8292F`), nitrate ochre (`#C87A19`), Prussian archival blue (`#234B6E`), and oxidised verdigris (`#2A6B53`), set in `Spectral` for film titles and `Source Sans 3` with tabular numerals for UI and credit ledgers, bounded by 1px warm stone hairlines (`#D8D0C0`).

STORY: The viewer sees their focal film anchored at the center of a warm vellum constellation or critical ledger, understands immediately which human collaborators and narrative tropes bind each recommended title through a proportional 6-segment craft lineage bar, and clicks directly to pivot the archive or stream the title in their region.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-viewport three-zone archival workspace: a 56px top catalogue bar with spine-style brand mark, global search input, view switcher (3D Constellation, 2D Map, Critical Ledger), and region selector; a streamlined 264px left curatorial rail housing Media Format tabs, the three Weighting Lenses, a single Minimum Match cutoff control, and Entity Layer toggles; the central warm-parchment spatial canvas (`#F6F1E7`) with engraved meridian rings and ink-on-paper node plates; and a 396px right Monograph Inspector sheet displaying poster plate, inline critical consensus strip, synopsis, regional watch links, and shared craft credits without nested card boxes.
